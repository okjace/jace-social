# Jace Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** Sep 26 2026 at 8:00 AM
**Last updated:** Oct 19 2026

This explains what information Jace ("we", "us") collects, why, who it's shared with, and what control you have. Jace is a hobby project run by an individual (Jace (or ququoqu)). Contact: jaceofficialbrand@hotmail.com.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information**
- Your **username** and a **password hash** (we never store your password itself; it's hashed with bcrypt).
- An **email address**, only if you choose to add one, plus whether it's verified.
- If you sign in with **GitHub**: your GitHub account ID and, depending on what GitHub shares, your username and email. We don't get your GitHub password.
- If you turn on **two-factor authentication**: the secret used to generate your codes.
- Your account settings, such as notification preferences and appearance (your theme, color theme, custom CSS, and any background color or image you upload). Appearance settings and background images are only shown to you.

**Profile information (public unless noted)**
- Bio, website, social links, avatar, and banner image, if you add them.
- Follower/following relationships.
- Game scores and leaderboard entries.

**Content you create**
- Videos (and thumbnails), projects and their versions, files and source code, issues and comments.
- Forum topics and replies, live chat messages, group chat messages, and **direct messages** (including any attachments).
- Files you store in **Cloud**, which are private to your account.
- Videos, games, live chat, direct messages, group chats, Cloud, organizations, and most forums are no longer shown on the site, but what you put in them is **kept, not deleted**. You can still delete any of it from "Your data".
- Content you post on behalf of an **organization**.

**Moderation and safety information**
- **Reports** you file (who reported, what was reported, the reason and details, and a short snapshot of the reported content).
- **Anonymous reports:** if you report without an account, we store a **salted, one-way hash of your IP address** (not the IP itself) so we can limit spam and duplicate reports.
- Ban and mute status, with the reason and time. If one of Jace's own apps bans or unbans an account, we also log which app did it, when, and why.

**Technical information**
- **Cookies** (see Section 4) and standard server logs that our hosting provider keeps, such as IP address, browser, and requested pages.

We do **not** use advertising trackers or third-party analytics on the site.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the site: sign you in, show your profile and content, deliver messages and notifications, and enforce settings like privacy and permissions.
- To send **emails** you ask for or need (email verification, password reset, sign-in links, account-deletion confirmation, and notifications you've enabled).
- To keep the community safe: review reports, moderate content, and stop abuse and spam.
- To secure the site and fix problems.

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see your information

- **The public / other users:** your username, profile, and any content you post publicly (videos, projects, forum posts, live chat, comments). "Unlisted" videos are visible to anyone with the link.
- **Recipients:** direct and group messages are visible to the people in that conversation.
- **Site administrators:** because they operate the site and its database, admins can technically access stored data, including direct messages, unpublished/private content, and email addresses. We only look at private content when needed to run the site, handle a report, or investigate abuse or security problems.
- **Organizations:** members and admins can see and manage content posted under their organization.
- **Apps you sign in to with Jace:** they get your username and avatar, plus your email address if you allow it, and whether your account is banned or muted. If you allow it, they can also see your game scores, and leaderboards show your username, avatar, and best scores to other players. Jace's own apps (such as Jace Games) can also save new scores to your account.
- **Jace's own apps** (marked Official, such as Jace Social): they can sign you in without asking each time. If one asks for your messages when you sign in, it can read your Jace direct messages and group chats and send messages as you, for example to bring your conversations into that app or keep them in sync. You can disconnect any app under Connected apps in your account settings, which stops its access right away.
- **Legal:** we may disclose information if required by law or to protect people's safety or the site.

## 4. Cookies

Jace uses only cookies needed for the site to work:

- **Session cookie**: keeps you signed in.
- **Security cookies**: protect sign-in and forms from forgery.
- **READ ME cookie**: if you're signed out and dismiss the READ ME popup, a small cookie remembers that so it doesn't keep reappearing. It only stores a timestamp.
- **Saved-accounts cookie**: if you use the account switcher, remembers the accounts you've signed into in that browser so you can switch between them. It's HTTP-only and only lists accounts you signed into yourself. You can remove an account from it with the trash button, or sign out.
- **Adding-account cookie**: when you open the account switcher's Add account popup, a short-lived HTTP-only cookie (it expires after 30 minutes, or as soon as you finish or cancel) remembers that you're adding an account, so signing in with GitHub, an emailed link, or a new sign-up still gets saved to the switcher. It only stores the value "1".

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. You can clear cookies in your browser at any time; you'll just be signed out.

## 6. How long we keep things

- Your account and content are kept **until you delete them or your account.**
- **Deleting your account** permanently deletes your profile, videos, projects, and scores, and removes you from organizations. Some things may remain: messages and posts other people already received or replied to may remain visible in their own copies or conversations, and copies in provider backups can persist for a limited time until they age out.
- **Muted/banned accounts:** their content is hidden, **not deleted**, so it can be restored if the restriction is lifted. Deleting the account still removes it.
- **Reports** and their content snapshots are kept as long as needed for moderation and to spot repeat abuse.
- Hosting/server logs are kept by Vercel under their own retention policy.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Edit** your profile, username, email, password, notification preferences, appearance, and two-factor settings in your account settings.
- **Disconnect** any app you signed in to with Jace under Connected apps.
- **Delete** your account from your profile settings (see above). With no email on file, you confirm with your password.
- **Delete any data you want at any time in your settings.** The "Your data" section of your profile settings has a delete button for each kind of data (videos, projects, forum posts, comments, chat and direct messages you sent, Cloud files, scores, likes/follows/notifications, and profile info), and you can also delete individual items directly.
- **Ask** us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, or for a correction or deletion we can't do ourselves: jaceofficialbrand@hotmail.com.
- Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights such as access, portability, correction, deletion, and objecting to certain processing. Contact us and we'll do our best to honor valid requests.

## 8. Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use signed and encrypted tokens, and traffic to the site uses HTTPS. But no system is perfectly secure, and Jace is a small hobby project. **Direct messages are not end-to-end encrypted.** Please don't share sensitive information (passwords, financial details, government IDs) through the site.

## 9. Children

Jace has **no minimum age**, so people of any age may have an account. We collect the same limited information from everyone (see Section 1) and don't use it for advertising. A parent or guardian who wants to see, correct, or delete a child's information or account can contact us at jaceofficialbrand@hotmail.com, and we'll help.
